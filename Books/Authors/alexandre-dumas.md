# Alexandre Dumas

**Biography:**  
Alexandre Dumas (1802–1870) was a prolific French writer, best known for historical adventure novels such as The Count of Monte Cristo and The Three Musketeers. His works have been translated into many languages and adapted for stage and screen.

**Major Works:**  
- [The Count of Monte Cristo](../the-count-of-monte-cristo-novel-summary.md)
- The Three Musketeers
- Twenty Years After
- The Man in the Iron Mask
