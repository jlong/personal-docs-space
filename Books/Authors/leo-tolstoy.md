# Leo Tolstoy

**Biography:**  
Leo Tolstoy (1828–1910) was a Russian writer regarded as one of the greatest novelists of all time. His works explore themes of morality, faith, and the search for meaning. He is renowned for his epic works War and Peace and Anna Karenina.

**Major Works:**  
- [War and Peace](../war-and-peace-novel-summary.md)
- [Anna Karenina](../anna-karenina-summary.md)
- The Death of Ivan Ilyich
- Resurrection
