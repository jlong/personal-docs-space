# Jane Austen

**Biography:**  
Jane Austen (1775–1817) was an English novelist known for her biting social commentary and masterful use of irony. Her works critique the British landed gentry at the end of the 18th century and feature strong, complex female protagonists. Austen's novels remain widely read and adapted today.

**Major Works:**  
- [Pride and Prejudice](../pride-and-prejudice-novel-summary.md)
- Sense and Sensibility
- Emma
- Mansfield Park
- Northanger Abbey
- Persuasion
