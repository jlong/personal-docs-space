# F. Scott Fitzgerald

**Biography:**  
F. Scott Fitzgerald (1896–1940) was an American novelist and short story writer. His works capture the spirit of the Jazz Age, chronicling the hedonism and recklessness of 1920s America. He is best known for his novel The Great Gatsby.

**Major Works:**  
- [The Great Gatsby](../the-great-gatsby-summary.md)
- This Side of Paradise
- Tender Is the Night
- The Beautiful and Damned
