# Emily Brontë

**Biography:**  
Emily Brontë (1818–1848) was an English novelist and poet, best known for her only novel Wuthering Heights, now considered a classic of English literature. Her work stands out for its passion and dark romanticism.

**Major Works:**  
- [Wuthering Heights](../wuthering-heights-novel-summary.md)
- Poems by Currer, Ellis, and Acton Bell
