# Mark Twain

**Biography:**  
Mark Twain (Samuel Langhorne Clemens, 1835–1910) was an American writer, humorist, and lecturer, celebrated for his wit and insight into American society. His novels, especially The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn, are considered classics of American literature.

**Major Works:**  
- [The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn](../the-adventures-of-huckleberry-finn-summary.md)
- The Adventures of Tom Sawyer
- A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur’s Court
- The Prince and the Pauper
