# Gustave Flaubert

**Biography:**  
Gustave Flaubert (1821–1880) was a French novelist regarded as one of the leading exponents of literary realism. He is best known for his first novel, Madame Bovary, which provoked both scandal and admiration for its unflinching look at bourgeois boredom and desire.

**Major Works:**  
- [Madame Bovary](../madame-bovary-novel-summary.md)
- Sentimental Education
- Salammbô
- Three Tales
