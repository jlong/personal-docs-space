# Fyodor Dostoevsky

**Biography:**  
Fyodor Dostoevsky (1821–1881) was a Russian novelist, philosopher, and journalist. His works delve into the psychology of the human soul, exploring faith, doubt, and morality. He is best known for his novels Crime and Punishment and The Brothers Karamazov.

**Major Works:**  
- [Crime and Punishment](../crime-and-punishment-summary.md)
- [The Brothers Karamazov](../the-brothers-karamazov-summary.md)
- Notes from Underground
- The Idiot
- Demons
