# Victor Hugo

**Biography:**  
Victor Hugo (1802–1885) was a French poet, novelist, and dramatist of the Romantic movement. He is one of the greatest French writers, best known for Les Misérables and The Hunchback of Notre-Dame. His work addresses social injustice and human rights.

**Major Works:**  
- [Les Misérables](../les-miserables-summary.md)
- The Hunchback of Notre-Dame
- The Last Day of a Condemned Man
