# Charles Dickens

**Biography:**  
Charles Dickens (1812–1870) was an English writer and social critic who created some of the world’s best-known fictional characters. His works depict the struggles of the poor in Victorian England and are famed for their vivid storytelling and social commentary.

**Major Works:**  
- [Great Expectations](../great-expectations-summary.md)
- [A Tale of Two Cities](../a-tale-of-two-cities-summary.md)
- Oliver Twist
- David Copperfield
- Bleak House
