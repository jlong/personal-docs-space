# Charlotte Brontë

**Biography:**  
Charlotte Brontë (1816–1855) was an English novelist and poet, the eldest of the three Brontë sisters whose novels became classics of English literature. Writing under the pen name Currer Bell, she is best known for her novel Jane Eyre, an innovative critique of Victorian domestic and religious ideals.

**Major Works:**  
- [Jane Eyre](../jane-eyre-summary.md)
- Shirley
- Villette
- The Professor
