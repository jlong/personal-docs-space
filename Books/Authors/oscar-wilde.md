# Oscar Wilde

**Biography:**  
Oscar Wilde (1854–1900) was an Irish playwright, poet, and novelist famed for his wit, flamboyant style, and scathing social commentary. He is particularly celebrated for his novel The Picture of Dorian Gray and his stage comedies.

**Major Works:**  
- [The Picture of Dorian Gray](../the-picture-of-dorian-gray-novel-summary.md)
- The Importance of Being Earnest
- An Ideal Husband
- De Profundis
